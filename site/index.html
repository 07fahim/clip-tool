How a clip gets out

  1. Pick

    The owner watches each stream and marks the moments worth keeping.

  2. Cut

    Whisper writes the captions and ffmpeg cuts a 9:16 clip with the streamer’s camera.

  3. Upload

    The ViralPulse uploader sends the clip to YouTube at the time the owner set.

  4. Post

    Facebook takes the clip through its Page API. Instagram is posted by hand.
